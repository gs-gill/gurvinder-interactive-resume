# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Gurvinder Gill for experienced data engineering roles are the primary audience. They need to understand his relevant work quickly and verify it against his resume. Technical interviewers are a secondary audience who may want more implementation detail.

## Product Purpose

Present Gurvinder's data engineering experience, technical skills, results, education, and contact details in a portfolio that supports hiring screening. A successful visit makes his fit for an experienced data engineering role clear and gives the visitor a direct path to the resume or contact information.

## Positioning

Reliable production data pipelines are the central story. The portfolio connects that focus to specific work: event-driven ingestion, backup and recovery, workflow automation, and production support at Tesco, alongside earlier migration, performance, healthcare, connected-equipment, and transaction-data projects.

## Operating Context

Visitors commonly arrive from a job application or shared link and may scan the page quickly before reading details. The primary page is directly navigable by section. An optional interactive park offers another way to explore the same work. The site is published through GitHub Pages.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The existing implementation is a static HTML/CSS/JavaScript site with no build step or framework.
- The main page links to experience, skills, about, contact, the latest resume PDF, and the optional interactive park.
- The park has map and menu navigation, keyboard and touch driving controls, and work stops including Experience.
- Professional claims and metrics must stay grounded in the supplied resume and known work history; do not invent projects, employers, testimonials, or results.
- Copy should sound like Gurvinder's own account of his work, using concrete descriptions rather than generic promotional claims.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Gurvinder Gill.
- The professional identity is an experienced data engineer based in Bengaluru, India.
- Preserve a straightforward, credible voice suitable for human and automated portfolio screening.

## Evidence on Hand

- `gurvinder-resume-2026-1.pdf` is the latest supplied resume and the source for experience, education, skills, and quantified results.
- `index.html` contains the primary portfolio; `park.html` contains the optional interactive experience.
- The existing content covers Tesco, Tiger Analytics, and Fragma Data Systems. No independent case-study documents, testimonials, or project screenshots are supplied.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the real work of building and operating dependable data systems.
2. Make role, technology, and outcome easy to scan without hiding the underlying detail.
3. Keep every professional claim traceable to supplied evidence.
4. Make the resume and contact paths easy to find.
